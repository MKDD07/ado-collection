About The Villa

Situated just minutes from the pristine shores of Utorda and Majorda beaches, this elegant 3BHK villa is designed to provide a perfect balance of luxury, comfort, and convenience. Ideal for vacations or workations, the villa’s serene setting and thoughtfully crafted amenities make it an excellent choice for travelers seeking relaxation and productivity in Goa.

The villa features spacious and beautifully appointed rooms with classic interiors, ensuring a cosy and sophisticated ambiance. Each room is designed to provide maximum comfort, making it the perfect retreat after a day of exploring the vibrant surroundings or relaxing by the pool. Whether you're traveling with family, friends, or for work, these rooms are tailored to meet your every need.

Adding to the villa’s charm is a private plunge pool, offering a secluded space to unwind and enjoy refreshing dips. For those seeking a larger space to relax and swim, the property also includes access to a sprawling common pool. Whether you’re soaking up the sun or enjoying a quiet evening swim, the pools add a luxurious touch to your stay.

The villa is equipped with a lift, providing easy access to all areas, making it convenient for guests of all ages. Its excellent location ensures easy accessibility to nearby attractions, best beaches in South Goa, and restaurants, allowing you to experience the best of the place effortlessly.

Heartfelt Hospitality & Gated Society

Rooted in "Atithi Devo Bhava", a dedicated 5-star team ensures exceptional service and heartfelt hospitality throughout your stay.

The gated society surrounding the villa offers a serene and secure environment, perfect for peaceful evening walks. Stroll through the lush pathways, breathe in the fresh air, and enjoy the quiet charm of the locality, enhancing your overall holiday experience.

Spaces, Common Areas & Swimming Pools

Thoughtfully laid out across multiple levels with an elevator connecting floors, Kensho Villas Hibiscus offers versatile social zones and private relaxation corners.

Living Room (260 sq. ft, Ground Floor): 55" TV on console, L-shaped sectional sofa with table, two armchairs, statement crystal chandelier, framed wall art, table lamps, two ceiling fans, potted plants, and direct garden access.
Dining Area & Powder Room: 6-seater wooden dining table, pendant lighting, garden view, and guest powder room located near the living area.
Modern Kitchen (Ground Floor): Fully equipped with high-end appliances: oven, microwave, gas cooktop, chimney, refrigerator, water purifier/RO, toaster, mixer/grinder, electric kettle, cookware, and full crockery/cutlery.
Common Swimming Pool (Ground Floor): 4.5 ft depth with kids pool, surrounded by trees, stone-paved pathway, well-manicured lawn, wooden lounge chairs & tables, and mesh canopy net. Timings: 7 AM - 10 PM.
Private Mini Plunge Pool (Terrace): Tiled plunge pool with two outdoor sofas, coffee table, and lush garden views surrounded by tropical palm trees.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your stay.

What are the check-in and check-out timings?
Check-in starts from 02:00 PM onwards, and check-out is by 11:00 AM. Early check-in or late check-out is subject to availability.
Is the villa senior citizen friendly?
Yes, Kensho Villas Hibiscus features an in-villa private lift connecting all floors, ground-floor living & dining areas, and accessible bathroom designs.
What swimming pools are available?
Guests have exclusive access to a private mini plunge pool on the terrace, as well as full access to the 4.5 ft common swimming pool and kids pool in the society (timings: 7 AM - 10 PM).
How close is the villa to the beach?
The villa is located just 1.6 km from Utorda Beach, 1.9 km from Majorda Beach, and 2.8 km from Sunset Beach.
